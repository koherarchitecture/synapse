## Why Koher and SDC use small models

Koher / SDC use small models for the following reasons:

- memory / deployment / inference cost
- syntax of intelligence - split domain cognition
- fast
- less expensive to train
- more control on the kind of logical steps performed when
- less black-box

Open problems:

- reading is still built on copyright-violations

## Steps

- identify what function of “intelligence” has to be performed
- break it down into a series or parallel arrays of singular operations
- figure if each of those singular operations can be performed by a small model
- identify the kind of data needed to train such a model
- build the model

## What small means

- size
- inference hardware needed should be free of cost
